Global Military Power Analytics Dashboard — Power BI
Business Problem
No unified, visual tool existed to compare military strength across countries using standardized, multi-domain metrics. Defense analysts and researchers had to rely on scattered data sources with no single source of truth.
Tools Used
Power BI (DAX, Power Query) · Data Modeling · Excel · ETL
Dataset

49 countries analyzed
70+ military metrics across 7 domains: Manpower, Land, Air, Naval, Technology, Logistics, Nuclear

What I Built

Multi-page Power BI report with maps, KPIs, bar charts, scatter plots, and ranking tables
30+ DAX measures including composite ranking indices and Top-N filters
Structured data model with relationships between all 7 domain tables
Geo-spatial visuals for regional defense distribution


Key Findings
Global Overview

USA leads defense spending at $997 Billion — largest globally
China has the world's largest standing army at 2.1 million active soldiers
India ranks 2nd in active forces at 1.5 million

Land Power

Russia leads in tanks (12,500 MBTs) and rocket artillery
USA leads in armored vehicle modernization and battle systems

Air Power

USA has 15,000+ helicopter units — unmatched air mobility
USA, China, and Turkey lead in drone/UAV capability

Naval Power

USA operates 11 aircraft carriers — no other nation comes close
China has the largest submarine fleet (largest count globally)
All US submarines are nuclear-powered

Nuclear

Russia holds the largest nuclear stockpile (~47% of global total)
USA, Russia, China have full nuclear triad (land + sea + air delivery)

Overall Conclusion: USA, China, Russia, and India consistently dominate across all 7 military domains.
